Artifacts

Eight pieces that pitch Static Harbor, each with its concept, execution and software.

Artifact 1 of 8

Key Art

Concept
The hero image that sells the game in one frame: a flooded service corridor at 2AM, a pink emergency backlight, and the Drifter standing between the player and the exit. It sets the palette (navy, cyan, hot pink) and the CRT security-camera look used across the whole pitch.
Execution
Built as a layered 1920x1080 PSD in five named groups: Environment, Lighting, Character, Typography and Effects. The pink backlight and cyan floor wash are blurred fills set to Screen blend, a shade layer anchors the title, and a scanline layer gives the camera-feed texture. The layered PSD keeps every element editable.
Software
Photoshop

Artifact 2 of 8

Logo & Character Sheet

Concept
Turnaround-style cards for the four enemies, the Tidewalkers, each with a silhouette that reads at a glance and one signature colour: the Drifter (cyan), Brine (amber), the Lamplighter (pink) and Undertow (bone). Each card carries a one-line behaviour caption so the AI rules are visible in the design.
Execution
Every figure is built from vector paths, ellipses and arcs on its own named layer, with the logo type on a separate layer. The palette is limited to five colours so the characters stay consistent with the key art. Saved as an AI file and exported as SVG and PNG.
Software
Illustrator

Artifact 3 of 8

Game Design Pitch Document

Open PDF

Concept
An eight-page pitch for a publisher or teammate: premise, core loop, systems, enemies, night-by-night progression, art and audio direction, and audience and scope. It is the written backbone that the other artifacts illustrate.
Execution
Built on a master page with the background, accent bar and an automatic page-number footer. Paragraph styles (Title, Body, Caption, Card Heading, Callout) and named colour swatches keep the type consistent, the progression chart is a real InDesign table, and images are linked placed files. Exported to PDF.
Software
InDesign

Artifact 4 of 8

Animated Camera-Feed Sequence

If no video appears, add media/04-animation.mp4

Concept
A 12-second looping animation of the security-camera feed as a Tidewalker approaches: a sonar ring spreads, the Drifter steps closer in three jumps, the feed breaks into glitch bars and NO SIGNAL, then the clock ticks to 3:13 AM and the loop restarts. It shows how the UI itself creates tension.
Execution
A layered Animate timeline at 24 fps with separate layers for the background, sonar ring (a symbol with a classic tween on scale and alpha), the Drifter's three scale jumps, glitch bars, NO SIGNAL flicker, clock and camera label, a blinking REC dot and a scanline overlay.
Software
Animate

Artifact 5 of 8

Teaser Trailer

If no video appears, add media/05-trailer.mp4

Concept
A 45-second teaser that follows one night from 12AM to 6AM: a title card, the key art push-in, camera views with the clock advancing, the glitch animation, quick cuts of the four Tidewalkers, a cut to black, and the dawn bell over the logo.
Execution
A 24 fps sequence assembled from the key art, scene stills, the animated sequence and the character cards, cut to the audio mix: a slow build to a hard cut to silence, then the bell. The cut follows a shot-by-shot plan with timing for every beat.
Software
Premiere Pro

Artifact 6 of 8

Ambient Audio & SFX Mix

If no player appears, add media/06-audio.mp3

Concept
The sound of the station in 75 seconds: a constant tide hum, steel creaks, sonar pings, radio static, wet footsteps and pipe knocks building to a sudden silence, then a dawn bell. The mix shows how audio warns the player before the danger arrives.
Execution
Seven separate tracks (tide hum, steel creak, sonar ping, radio static, footsteps, pipe knocks, dawn bell) arranged on a multi-track Audacity timeline, with the silence and bell placed at the end of the build. Exported as MP3.
Software
Audacity

Artifact 7 of 8

In-Game UI Mockup

Concept
A full in-game screen at the 3:12 AM mark of Night 3: the clock panel, a power meter, and Gate A, Gate B and Sonar controls laid over the office view. It shows the systems the player juggles all at once.
Execution
A layered PSD with grouped HUD elements (Clock, Power, Controls), each panel, bar and label on its own layer so any state, such as a different power level, can be edited in seconds. A scanline layer matches the camera look of the rest of the pitch.
Software
Photoshop

Artifact 8 of 8

Poster & HUD Icon Set

Concept
A promotional poster with the Lamplighter lit by a pink glow, plus four HUD icons (clock, floodgate, sonar and power) that reuse the game's colour coding.
Execution
Vector artwork on named layers: background glow, character, typography and the icon set. The icons are drawn on a shared grid with a consistent stroke weight. Saved as an AI file and exported as SVG and PNG.
Software
Illustrator
