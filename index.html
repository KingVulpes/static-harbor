STATIC HARBOR

Welcome. This portfolio is the pitch for Static Harbor, an original survival-horror game. Six hours. One control room. Something is coming up through the tide.

View the artifactsSite wireframe

The game

You are the night operator of a failing tidal-control station. From 12AM to 6AM you watch the cameras, manage power, work the floodgates and track sonar pings while the Tidewalkers, drowned figures that move through the harbor on unpredictable routes, try to reach your office.

Genre

Survival horror, resource management, visual novel presentation

Core loop

Watch, ration power, close gates, survive until 6AM, unlock the next night

Look

Pseudo-3D scenes in a 2D engine using depth-map lighting, neon-cyan and hot-pink palette

Engine

Ren'Py with custom shaders and Python scripting

Audience

Fans of tense, replayable horror and story-driven indies

Scope

Five nights, endless mode, four Tidewalker types

Design pillars

Unique every run

Randomised enemy routes and timings so no night plays the same

Depth from flat art

Depth-map deferred lighting gives 2D images real shadows

Clock as pressure

A visible 12AM to 6AM clock and timed events drive the tension
